Smart pop-ups for medical appointments

A proactive system that brings more flow, context and productivity to the daily work of healthcare professionals at Conexa.

Product DesignUX ResearchPrototypingHealthcareDashboardProductivity

Project context

Conexa is a digital health platform with thousands of medical appointments every day. The challenge was the low use of booked time slots and how hard it was for clinicians to see their upcoming appointments.

As Product Designer, I worked with the PM, developers and health team stakeholders to create a solution that brought more flow and productivity to clinicians’ daily work.

Main goals

  • Reduce friction before each appointment
  • Make better use of booked time slots
  • Increase clinician occupancy
  • Make the system proactive and smart

Problems identified

  • Clinicians didn’t know who to see next
  • Confusing navigation between schedule, waiting room and medical record
  • No indicators such as “first appointment” or ICD code
  • No suggested actions from the system

These problems got in the way of a smooth journey, directly affecting clinicians’ productivity and satisfaction.

Methodology

  1. Flow mappingIn-depth review of the existing dashboards to find friction points in the clinician’s journey.
  2. Journey modelingOptimized user flows for different appointment contexts.
  3. Prototyping in FigmaWorking prototypes focused on the user experience.
  4. Rule documentationDetailed specification of business logic to align with engineering.
  5. Cross-functional collaborationClose work with stakeholders to ensure technical and business feasibility.

Solution: a proactive pop-up system

I designed a proactive pop-up system that appears automatically a few minutes before each booked appointment. The pop-up has two main states: collapsed and expanded.

Collapsed stateShows quick details such as patient name, time and appointment type without getting in the way.
Expanded stateLets the clinician start the appointment, delay it or open the patient’s record.
Persistent modalAdapts to the clinician’s context and preferences.

Key features

  • A persistent, smart modal that adapts to context
  • Adaptable logic for different appointment types
  • Interaction tracking for continuous improvement
  • Suggested actions based on the appointment context
  • Built-in visual tags for quick identification
  • A timer component for precise time control

Prototypes and components

The system was designed to be intuitive and efficient, with reusable components and logic that adapts to clinical workflows.

The components were built for usability and for smooth integration with Conexa’s existing system.

Results

1.71→2.6Booked appointments per hour, per clinician
30%Fewer delays with contextual alerts
200k+Monthly appointments affected by the new logic
1.2hPotential time saved per clinician each week

Additional impact

  • The feature was sold to other companies that use scheduling tools
  • More efficient clinician occupancy
  • The product team now uses the component as the base for new smart queues
  • A significantly better experience for healthcare professionals

Final thoughts

This project showed how simple, smart solutions can solve complex problems. The proactive pop-up system improved clinician productivity and created a solid base for future innovation on the platform.

Its success confirmed how important it is to deeply understand users’ workflows and design solutions that fit naturally into their routines, creating real, measurable value.

The solution became a competitive advantage for Conexa and was even sold to other digital health companies.
