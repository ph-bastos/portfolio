Notification center

More clarity, speed and confidence across the healthcare professional’s journey.

UX ResearchPrototypingDesign SystemUsabilityHealthcareDashboard

Introduction

Healthcare professionals deal every day with appointments, rescheduling, patient messages and critical updates. Before the notification center, this information was scattered, forcing them to jump between screens and raising the risk of missing something important.

The center was created to bring alerts, messages and pending actions into a single place, making work smoother and the experience more reliable.

Problem

  • Low visibility of critical updates
  • A fragmented experience with data spread across many areas
  • Higher cognitive load to manage simple tasks
  • Risk of errors and forgotten tasks without a central system

Hypotheses

  • A notification center could reduce the time spent on administrative tasks
  • Centralized alerts would help increase engagement with the platform
  • The component could improve clinician retention and trust

Project goals

  • Make important information easy to reach in real time
  • Reduce friction in how clinicians manage their tasks
  • Improve the platform’s usability and predictability
  • Increase the sense of organization and reliability

Methodology

  1. Current journey mappingA detailed review of the flow without a notification center, identifying friction points and lost information.
  2. BenchmarkingA study of other health systems and SaaS products to find best practices for centralizing alerts.
  3. Stakeholder interviewsConversations with product, support and CX teams to understand internal needs.
  4. PrototypingA Figma prototype focused on predictability and ease of use.
  5. ValidationUsability tests with real users to refine the proposal.
  6. KPI planningEngagement and efficiency metrics defined for after the release.

Shipped version

The final solution brings every notification into an accessible, contextual component, with a clear information hierarchy and direct actions for the most common tasks.

The design prioritizes visual clarity, with alert categories and quick access to the actions clinicians use most.

Impact

+37%Average notification interactions per session
25%Less time to reschedule appointments
NPS+Better NPS on organization and usability

Key achievements

  • 37% more average notification interactions per session after launch
  • On average, 25% less time to reschedule appointments with direct access from the center
  • Positive qualitative feedback pointing to more predictability and control in daily work
  • An upward NPS trend, especially in mentions of organization and usability
  • A structured base for smart alerts and future AI-driven automation

Final thoughts

This project showed how small improvements in how information is organized can have a big impact on the user experience. Centralizing notifications reduced clinicians’ cognitive load and created a solid base for the product’s next steps.

Its success confirmed the value of listening to users’ real needs and turning research insights into practical, effective solutions.
